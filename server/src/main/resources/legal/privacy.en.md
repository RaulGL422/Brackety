# Privacy policy

Version {{VERSION}}

This policy explains which personal data Brackety processes, why, for how long and how to exercise your rights. Brackety is a platform to organize sports leagues, leagues in phases and tournaments.

## 1. Controller

- Controller: {{OWNER_NAME}} (individual).
- Privacy contact: {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}.

When a club or organizer enters its students into an event, the organizer decides which of their data is used and must have their permission or their guardians'. In that case Brackety processes that data on the organizer's behalf, only to run the event.

## 2. Data we process

**If you have an account**
- Email, password (stored with irreversible hashing) and language.
- If you sign in with Google: the identifier Google gives your account and your Google email.
- Profile: date of birth; if you are an organizer, the organization's name and tax ID (CIF).
- Security data: IP address and sign-in attempts, to stop attacks.

**If you take part in an event**
- First name, last names, date of birth and approximate height.
- For minors without an account: the organizer's confirmation that the guardian agrees.
- Your sports activity at the event: groups, matches, points, warnings and standings.

We do not process health or injury data. **Brackety does not handle payments**: it can only record whether an entry is paid or not; payment happens outside the app.

## 3. Purposes and legal basis

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Create and keep your account, sign in | Performance of the contract (terms of use) |
| Run events: entries, groups, scoreboard, standings | Performance of the contract with the organizer; guardian consent for minors |
| Account emails (verification, recovery, security alerts) | Performance of the contract |
| Security: lockout after failed attempts, usage limits | Legitimate interest in protecting the service |
| Reading student lists when importing them (photos or files) | Performance of the contract with the organizer, who confirms having the guardians' permission |

We do not use your data for advertising or build commercial profiles.

## 4. Minors

- A minor under 14 cannot create their own account: their profile is managed by a guardian or by the club with the guardian's permission.
- A club can only enter a minor without an account if it confirms that their guardian agrees.
- Student photos, if any, are never public.

## 5. Who we share data with

Only with providers we need to run the service (processors), under contract and safeguards:

- **Cloudflare**: network and secure access to the website.
- **Resend**: sending emails.
- **Google**: Google sign-in (if you use it).
- **Anthropic**: reading student lists when an organizer imports them. It only reads the list; it does not keep the files or use them to train models.

Some are in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Other participants and the public only see what the organizer publishes about the event (for example, standings). Photos and dates of birth are never published.

## 6. How long we keep it

- Unverified accounts: deleted after 7 days.
- Deleted account: 30 days to restore it; then it is deleted for good. Your results in past events stay anonymized.
- Sign-in attempts and IP: only while the lockout or the control window lasts.
- Event data: as long as the organizer needs it for the league or tournament.
- Encrypted backups: up to about 2 months.

## 7. Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction or object to processing. From the app settings you can **export your data** and **delete your account**. For anything else, write to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}.

If an organizer entered your data (or your child's), you can contact them or us; we will pass it on.

If you think we are not handling your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

## 8. Security

Encrypted connections, irreversibly hashed passwords, role-based access and encrypted backups. If there were a security breach with a risk to you, we would let you know.

## 9. Changes

If we change something important, we will ask you to accept this policy again. The current version is always on this page.
