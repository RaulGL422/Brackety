# Cookie policy

Version {{VERSION}}

Brackety only uses **technical** cookies and storage, needed for the service to work. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, so we do not ask for your consent for them. If we ever added a non-technical one, we would ask you first.

## What we use

| Name | Where | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `brackety_refresh` | Web (first-party cookie, readable only by the server) | Keep you signed in | Up to 30 days or until you sign out |
| Cloudflare technical cookies (e.g. `__cf_bm`) | Web | Protect the service from bots and attacks | Minutes or hours |
| Device secure storage | Android app | Keep you signed in and remember the chosen server | Until you sign out or uninstall the app |

## How to delete them

Signing out ends the session. You can also delete cookies from your browser settings; you will need to sign in again.
